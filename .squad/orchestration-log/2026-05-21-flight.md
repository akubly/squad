## Rationale for P0 Classification

Without transparent state isolation, org-deployed Squad fails basic developer-experience hygiene: every session generates noise in PR diffs that reviewers cannot evaluate and that may block merge through branch-protection policies. The friction actively undermines Squad adoption in settings where multi-squad management is most valuable.

The mechanism already exists (`stateBackend` config, orphan/two-layer backends). Elevating it to P0 ensures it is:
1. Treated as prerequisite for org deployment, not post-launch hardening
2. Documented in implementation plans
3. Validated in acceptance criteria
